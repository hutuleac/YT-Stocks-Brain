The one-paragraph takeaway

Video Snapshot

  1. Prenetics founder Danny Yeung launched IM8 (David Beckham co-founder) in Dec 2024; $100M run rate at 11 months, $200M at 18 months, and $20M monthly revenue last month.
  2. Positioning was the bet: the supplement aisle is green, so IM8 went red (beets gave the natural colour), with a premium red box, 90 ingredients replacing 16 supplements, $90/month vs $250-300.
  3. Credibility stack before launch: a scientific advisory board, a clinical trial, and two third-party tests (Eurofins, NSF Certified for Sport) — his answer to a skeptical, celebrity-brand-fatigued market.
  4. Growth engine: 2-3,000 live Meta ads, 500-800 creators tested weekly, a home-built creative-fatigue score; Q2 spend doubled ($17M to $33M) while CAC slipped from $305 to $301, then $239 in July.
  5. 200+ offline events (7 in 7 days in New York) are a deliberate bet that in-person trust is less contested in an age of AI fakes.
  6. The prior boom: Prenetics did $700M+ revenue and 28M PCR tests in three COVID years, listed on NASDAQ in 2022 at ~$1B, then fell ~95% to ~$50M market cap; he cut 2,000 staff the moment COVID ended.
  7. Founder mindset: telemarketing at 15 (100-200 calls a night, 90-95% rejection), involved in every key decision, alternate between delusion and realism.

Framework PRODUCT FIRST, THEN POSITIONINGconsumerhealth

Better product, red not green, and a science bench before any ad

Yeung's formula: a genuinely better product, taste-tested to death, in a visibly different category position, backed by credentialed science.

  • His board opposed the move into supplements; he told them he would resign if wrong — the Beckham commitment was not small.
  • Beckham pitch: he asked for a co-founder, not an ambassador, because the brand has to outlive him — avoiding another celebrity brand (more fail than succeed).
  • Beckham's problem he solved: even he and Victoria found supplements overwhelming, unable to keep up 12-15 pills while travelling.
  • Spent a week at the factory testing 20-30 flavours — if it doesn't taste good nobody drinks it twice.
  • Red, not green: beets in the formulation gave a natural red; he leaned in with a big red premium box and unboxing experience. Name: 'I am' + 8 flipped = infinity, and 8 is lucky in China.
  • Scientific advisory board: a Mayo Clinic physician, a former NASA chief scientist (a prior contact), and Cedars-Sinai's microbiome director; the Mayo physician was cold-contacted on LinkedIn.
  • Proof before launch: a clinical trial plus third-party testing by Eurofins and NSF Certified for Sport (free of 280 banned substances).

Recommendation Q2 VS Q1 — SPEND DOUBLED, CAC DOWNconsumer

Creative-volume ad machine, falling CAC and 200+ offline events

Meta handles targeting now; the edge is creative volume and diversity, plus in-person events that Yeung believes lower CAC.

  • 2-3,000 ads live at once, ~55-60% static and the rest video; 500-800 creators tested weekly; ~$300K+ spent per day on Meta.
  • Content comes from top ambassadors plus 1,000+ affiliates; a proprietary creative-fatigue score predicts when an ad's return will drop.
  • Q1: CAC $305, AOV about $230, marketing ~$17M; Q2: marketing doubled to $33M and CAC fell $4 to $301; July CAC $239 at $10M+ monthly spend.
  • 200+ offline events (7 in 7 days in New York) with advisors, formulators and doctors present; hard to scale but valuable 'in the age of AI' when you can't tell what's real.
  • He admits no quantifiable data attributing the CAC drop to events, but will keep doubling down.
  • Hosts' examples: Grüns runs ~8 persona/problem-specific ad-to-landing-page funnels simultaneously; Method soap's founder looks for a 'sea of sameness' (rectangular among round vitamin bottles).
  • China: IM8 would need a completely new team and model; he says AG1 isn't doing well there and 99% of US firms fail by exporting their model.

Cautionary tale PRENETICS: PEAK ~$1B VALUATION TO ~$50Mbiotechhealthfinance

From COVID PCR windfall to a 95% market-cap collapse, and the 2,000-person cut

Prenetics printed $700M+ in three years of PCR testing, then watched revenue fall ~95% and market cap go from over $1B to ~$50M.

  • April 2020: the Hong Kong government declined his lab offer, so he launched an at-home PCR kit in ~2 weeks at $100 while others charged $300-400.
  • July 2020: the government asked him to test 300,000 restaurant workers across 18,000 restaurants in ~3 weeks; he scaled from 200 to 10,000 tests/day and hired 2-3,000 people.
  • Totals: 28 million PCR tests, peak 40,000/day, $700M+ revenue, $100M+ net profit; revenue peaked at $272M and fell ~95%.
  • He says Prenetics was the only provider with no false positive/negative blunders, so airport arrivals were mandated to use its test.
  • NASDAQ listing 2022 at ~$1B valuation; 18 months later market cap ~$50M. He weighed closing and returning capital before choosing a new business.
  • Let go 2,000 people the moment COVID ended; Cue Health (peak ~$3B cap, ~$1B US revenue) and Lucira (~$2B cap) went bankrupt ~18 months post-COVID after overbuilding manufacturing and at-home testing.

Principle FROM TELEMARKETER AT 15 TO BILLION-DOLLAR VALUATIONScareermindset

Telemarketing, persistence and founder mode

Yeung credits early rejection-heavy sales and 'involved in everything' ownership for each of his pivots.

  • Dropped out of high school in San Francisco (kicked out for fighting); telemarketing timeshares at 15, 100-200 calls a night, ~90-95% rejection.
  • Career path: restaurant at 24 (3-4 years), then hotel furniture importing from China (a $4M MGM CityCenter contract after cold-calling MGM), then Hong Kong group-buying in 2010.
  • Groupon bought 51% of his Hong Kong e-commerce firm (YouBuy); he left in 2014 at ~$200M annual revenue.
  • Persistence story: a restaurant director hung up on him, so he called the boss (owner of 30+ restaurants), who signed all of them after meeting for 20 minutes.
  • Host story: Shaan's uncle Vinnie, door-to-door seller, put a price on 'no' ($100 yes / 20 nos = $5 each) and became the top salesman.
  • Founder vs hired CEO: founders come back after growing to ~$400M because hired CEOs are conservative and paycheck-minded.
  • Delusion vs realism: be delusional enough to start, hyper-realistic to execute; SEO story — be actually better, no hack required.

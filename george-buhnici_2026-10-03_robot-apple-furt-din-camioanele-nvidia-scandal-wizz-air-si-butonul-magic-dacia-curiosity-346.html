Leu at 5.35, diesel at 11 lei: a year of fiscal effort erased in days

The market bet against the leu all year, and the depreciation plus pricey diesel will hit prices fast.

  • EUR/RON at 5.35 interbank on recording day; the bar moved to 5.36-5.37, and "someone front-ran junk" ahead of the S&P rating.

  • Industry rumor: locally made gasoline is sold ~1 RON dearer to subsidize diesel, whose real price is closer to 13 RON; pump margins are thin, stations earn on coffee and shop sales.
  • The new TollRO road-toll system crashed on day one (fines waived); truck tolls reach ~€10,000 a year and will flow into transport costs.

Recommendation CERNAVODA — RESTART DELAYED TO NOVEMBER 15energypolicyromania

Cernavoda offline, energy contracts reset: let AI find your supplier

With Cernavoda down and contracts changing automatically, comparing offers with an AI assistant can save hundreds of lei.

  • The Cernavoda unit restart slipped again, from end-September to November 15; the plant covers up to 18% of domestic consumption, and the Danube's flow keeps falling.
  • An energy trader welcomed the delay: "to buy cheaper and sell dearer later."
  • Prosumers paid day-ahead exchange prices get ~0 at noon, when they produce most, and buy back at ~1.7 RON in the evening; the supplier resells their power to neighbors at contract price.
  • Tip of the week: give a year of gas and power bills to any AI (Google AI Mode, ChatGPT, Meta AI) and ask for the best offer — tariff data is public; Buhnici found a ~400 RON/month gap.
  • Watch netting rules: a hydro-only supplier can't offset against gas, so you need one that also sells gas.
  • The Prosumers and Energy Communities Association (president Pirsan) says apartment-block prosumer rules are close; Lidl balcony solar kits only work for self-consumption today.
  • Call to homeowners' associations (including the Habitat federation): push for the right to generate power in apartment blocks.

Micron earns an 86.8% margin, yet nobody is building new memory fabs

Memory makers prefer high prices to another overbuild, so the RAM shortage lasts for years.

  • Micron: estimated $54B revenue in Q4 alone and an 86.8% operating margin — "clearly someone should start making memory."
  • RAM supply was sold out for all of 2027 by summer, with orders booked into 2028, mostly for data centers and HBM.

  • AI agents add demand: each runs its own cloud computer with RAM; big labs even buy Mac Studios for local models.
  • Laptops: nothing decent under 4,000 RON anymore; Alex thinks Lenovo and HP still have options under 2,000 RON, mini PCs are the alternative; beware gaming laptops sold with DDR4.
  • Samsung workers took to the streets over guaranteed bonuses of ~100,000.

Names and terms at a glance

Glossary & Entities

EU261 — EU regulation requiring airlines to compensate and assist passengers for cancellations, delays and overbooking.
ANPC — Romania's National Authority for Consumer Protection, where complaints go alongside an EU261 claim.
BNR — Banca Nationala a Romaniei, the central bank that manages the leu's exchange rate.
Prosumer — A consumer who generates and feeds their own power, usually solar, into the grid.
HBM — High Bandwidth Memory, the wide-bandwidth memory used by AI accelerators.
Tau's law — Huawei's approach of gaining performance by cutting signal noise and stacking transistors rather than shrinking them.
Agentic commerce — Shopping done by AI agents that read websites and place orders for the user.
Post-quantum cryptography — Encryption algorithms designed to withstand quantum computers.
ADAS — Advanced driver-assistance systems (speed and lane warnings), increasingly mandated by the EU.
No-show — Passengers who don't turn up (at least 2%), the reason airlines sell over capacity.

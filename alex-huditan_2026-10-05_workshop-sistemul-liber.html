Framework TESTED JULY + AUGUST 2026 · ORGANIC ONLYcareer

Six steps, three tools: Facebook post → DM → Stripe → muted WhatsApp group → 10 days → offer

The whole sales stack is a personal Facebook profile, a copy-pasted DM template, a Stripe link and a WhatsApp group.

  • Steps: a week of launch posts, a private-message reply with a payment link, Stripe payment, automatic redirect into a muted WhatsApp group, 10 daily posts, then the upgrade offer.
  • Stripe trick: replace the confirmation page with a redirect to the WhatsApp group invite link, so people land in the group 'almost in the same second'. Join requests are approved manually against Stripe names.
  • DMs are answered by hand from a template in his phone's notes, lightly personalised. A personal profile can't be automated with ManyChat (pages and Instagram only), so it took hundreds of messages and several hours. He's considering delegating it.
  • Daily content: pre-scripted 2-7 minute videos, filmed rather than text so people 'spend time with you'. The format also filters out people who don't resonate. He started text-only in earlier years.
  • No company needed: without a legal entity, use Gumroad or Stan Store instead, at the cost of one extra page step.
  • Known gap he left on purpose ('I went lazy'): no reminder to people who DMed but never paid.

Counterintuitive take COMPLETION ~10% FREE → ~89% PAIDmindsetcareer

Charge for the challenge and kick out anyone who misses a day; completion jumps nearly ninefold

Paying plus a hard 24-hour rule turned a challenge most people abandoned into one almost everyone finished.

  • Free editions last year: 1,500+ sign-ups across about four editions, only 115 finished.
  • Paid editions: about 89% finished in July (137 sign-ups) and August (142 of 163).
  • Rule: react to his daily post within 24 hours or you're removed. A script he built with Claude flags the non-reactors. Kicked out: 15 in July, 21 in August.
  • The rule was a selling point because it was shocking ('I pay and you throw me out?'). The comment debate it caused helped the algorithm.
  • Some reactions were 'violent'. Privately he gave kicked-out people free entry to the next edition: 'in the background I was actually a decent guy'. One participant got back in the same day by sending her homework.
  • Upgrade offer: a Zoom 'graduation party' in July (13 sign-ups), then in August just a ~2-minute video plus testimonials, which converted better (19 + 1 full price).

Recommendation PERSONAL PROFILE > PAGE · STORY > PROMOcareerromania

Organic Facebook reach: storytelling posts, long reads, DMs not comments, and a thick skin

Facebook's algorithm now understands context and rewards new creators who add value, but it punishes posts that are only promotion.

  • The launch week was an 'emotional roller coaster' of story (why paid beats free, proof from July), not 'join my challenge, it's the best'.
  • One free-exercise post reached 200,000+ people: 700 reactions, 100 shares, 200 saves. Time on post matters most, so he writes longer posts on purpose.
  • Sign-ups by DM, not comments: commenting is low-intent, and replies from a non-friend land in a hidden folder. 'It's OK to be a bit harder' to sign up.
  • Use a personal profile, not a page: pages limit reach and push you to pay. A 'digital creator' profile mode makes no difference.
  • The price of exposure: abusive comments. He deletes and blocks rather than leaving them up for engagement, because negativity attracts more negativity.
  • Since the start of the year he has posted in Romanian after 10 years in English. Over 95% of buyers were new to him, and three years ago posts mostly reached friends only.
  • Use AI for structure and angles, never to write the posts for you.

Framework ~€25,000 OVER ~1.5-2 MONTHScareermindset

An ecosystem priced by proximity: cheap impulse entry, half-price scholarships for finishers, €7,777 for Bali one-to-one

The closer you get to him, the more you pay, and each tier is sold only to people who finished the one below.

  • Ladder: a planned ~€5 book, the ~€30 challenge, Reset Normal (€888 list), the 4-week Platit sa Exist cohort, the points-gated 'Enlightened Millionaires' group with retreats, and Bali one-to-one at €7,777 plus days with him.
  • Finishers get a 50% 'scholarship' for four days only. He says the deadline is deliberate psychological urgency, set just before the next edition starts.
  • He sold one Reset Normal cohort from two challenges: the July buyers waited almost a month, and the cohort started with 33 people.
  • Revenue totalled roughly €25,000, but a participant pointed out it took about two months, not one, against his €25,000-a-month target. He has lived at €25k/month for 10+ years, he says.
  • Entry is an impulse buy, like 'a pill for a pain they feel', not creatine you take daily. He says to sell the solution, not just the map.
  • Don't launch new products while one is working ('strike while the iron is hot'). Consistency keeps you in people's heads and drives referrals.
  • Platit sa Exist: 4 weeks, 2-4 hours a week, Monday framework videos, Thursday live calls, a WhatsApp accountability group and a custom GPT 'version of me'.

Principle OPINION · Q&A COACHINGmindsetrelationships

Sell your own process, broken into steps, from an 'energy of giving'

The product is what you lived and know works, made concrete. No niche is required, and AI-generated exercises 'steal power' from it.

  • No niche needed: talk from life experience (kids, work, 'what my mum told me'). He shows the strategy behind his work 'like a magician showing the trick'.
  • Coaching a participant about forgiveness: 'I learned detachment' is too vague. Make it step 1, 2, 3 (e.g. visualise the parent for 30 seconds, ho'oponopono), and give it away free first.
  • Vulnerability works as a before/after contrast. People buy where there is pain: relationships, money, health.
  • On privacy: frame it as a client's story, or ask the people involved first. Cautionary tale: a friend's ex kept a 'no badmouthing' pact to the letter but broke its spirit by posting about toxic relationships. Tony Robbins was cited for telling his mother's story lightly.
  • Comparison he draws: a Bucharest rental studio yields ~€300/month. Six months of consistent posting should be able to match that from a digital product.
  • Participant takeaways: 'simplify, simplify', launch imperfect and watch the reaction; and 'less is more' for someone who'd stayed busy to justify their existence.
  • Simple, not easy: 'if you think you can't, you're right'.

What to do with this

Best Actionable Takeaways

🛠
Creator business: Replace the Stripe confirmation page with a redirect into the delivery channel, so payment and onboarding are one step
⏱
Engagement: Add a cost and a hard participation rule to free programs before judging completion rates
📣
Distribution: Post from a personal profile as long-form story, and collect sign-ups by DM so only high-intent people come through
🪜
Pricing: Gate each upgrade to people who finished the previous tier, and give the offer a short, real deadline
🧭
Product: Turn your own process into numbered steps before selling it; keep AI to structure, not substance

Markets as a horse-racing odds board: bet the gap between your odds and the screen's

  • Son of a core driller who taught him horse handicapping, billiards, backgammon and chess; a book on compounding (10% a year for 50 years) pulled him into markets.

  • He reads conviction, not polls: at dinners he tallies the room's billions; when everyone with capital says 'never' on Bitcoin, the odds priced in are close to zero.
  • Bitcoin is 'the most asymmetric bet I've ever made' — a bet on fiat assets vs something with no fundamentals; Bayesian updating, no preconceived notions.
  • Traded against LTCM in Brazilian preferred vs ordinary shares in 1998 as the spread kept widening.

Ghost rails: crypto was built for humans who don't care — agents will use it

Jordi's bull case is '100% AI agents': software users will chase every spread banks live on, making crypto a margin-compression trade.

  • 'Bitcoin's silent IPO' (last year): OG whales selling (e.g. Galaxy's 80,000 BTC sale in July) mirror internet holders in 2000-03 and Google after 2004; government acceptance plus the ETF was the sell-the-news event, with altcoins and the SBR hype signalling it.
  • Ghost rails, after China's ghost cities (his 2006-12 trips, Macau cash flows): rails get built before the users arrive — and the users are agents, since wealthy people don't want faster wires.
  • Short friction: JPMorgan's net interest income of $107B since 2022 'shouldn't exist'; bank deposits up under 10% vs money market funds up 70%+. Stripe and Canton offer yield; Clarity failed, so no stablecoin-yield ban.
  • Crypto means cryptography: agent swarms and soaring hacking (Meta's Muse, OpenAI solving math problems) make provable ownership — even code IP on-chain — essential. Crypto is $3-4T against $900T of hackable, friction-laden assets.
  • Banks launching stablecoins = retailers launching websites against Amazon: a sign they feel the threat. Tech went from 13% of the S&P in 2007 to 56%; VC and private equity have cost money since 2017.
  • 24/7 markets: Korea aims for full tokenization by 2027, Japan too; Druckenmiller buying into Hyperliquid (oil traded there over the Iran weekend). Prime brokers will remark books on weekends — 'the destruction of hedge funds'.
  • Trust: crypto 'leads with a hoodie', but SVB's run and Bank of America's $1B+ fraud fine (stock -8%) show TradFi is no different.

Contrarian JORDI VISSER · NOT A BUBBLEai-inframacro-ratessemis

AI isn't a bubble — it's a concentration that rates are making brutal

No valuation, credit or supply bubble exists, but higher yields crush the zombie companies and the debt-dependent parts of the buildout.

  • Three bubble types: valuation (dot-com), credit/fraud (housing), oversupply. Nvidia at 15x earnings vs Cisco's 100x; no fraudulent lending; compute demand still exceeds supply.
  • Adoption gap: ~5% of office workers use agents vs ~50% in coding and engineering — demand for compute keeps growing as Muse and others spread.
  • Losers outnumber winners: homebuilders 'get screwed' as AI buildout crowds out borrowers; ZIRP-era zombies that issued 30-50 year debt suffer as rates rise.
  • First stress: the Neoclouds, since data centers are a financing, memory and chip-cost business; Google, Meta, Anthropic and OpenAI 'don't care'. Broadcom and Intel said capital is now the bottleneck.
  • AI debt is ~5-6% of the buildout; hyperscaler debt is tiny next to equity, and there's an asset side (compute, long-term contracts).
  • A 20% S&P correction is possible — there were ones in 2018, COVID, 2022 and 2025's tariffs, plus a 10% dip in February — but that isn't a bubble.
  • Memory stocks 'are going to be very important'; Korea is the overnight AI trade — US AI names gap on KOSPI moves and then barely move.

When AI kills every moat, Bitcoin's belief is the one left — and wealth becomes money

Jordi rejects 'debasement hedge': Bitcoin wins because competition compresses every company's multiple while belief, like gold's, endures.

  • Schumpeter (creative destruction ending in socialism), Milton Friedman and Ray Kurzweil's singularity: AI speeds disruption until no moat survives — hence UBI talk and presidents mailing checks.
  • Zombie incumbents: McDonald's asking franchisees to spend $1M remodelling while out-innovated and hit by Ozempic-style drugs.
  • Three lasting beliefs: gold (shells and tulips failed), religion, Santa Claus. First fiat — Marco Polo saw China's mulberry-bark money around 1200. Hundreds of millions chose Bitcoin as the store of value.
  • S&P up ~13% YTD while earnings grew ~33%, Anthropic ARR surprised, capex revised up — multiple compression means nobody trusts moats beyond 3 years. Bitcoin 'doesn't have to deal with that'.
  • Not a liquidity trade: Bitcoin has tracked, not beaten, the Mag 7 for eight years. Next phase: Mag 7 multiples compress while Bitcoin outperforms — equities are only $150T of the $900T in assets.
  • 'When wealth becomes money': tokenized property (a swap on your house's economics, Miami vs Maine) turns illiquid wealth into M2/M3, reducing the need to print — Lyn Alden's 'nothing stops this train' is the opposing view.
  • Second fix: tax corporations, since humanoids will be owned by the powerful, first adopters are quant funds and the Mag 7 (UnitedHealth is slow), and individuals move states.

Names and terms at a glance

Glossary & Entities

Four-year cycle — Bitcoin's historical pattern of a peak roughly every four years followed by a ~12-month bear market.
OG distribution — Selling by long-dormant early Bitcoin holders.
Net interest income — What a bank earns on loans and securities minus what it pays depositors.
Short friction — Jordi's thesis of betting against intermediaries' spreads, which agents and crypto rails compress.
Neoclouds — Newer GPU-cloud providers that finance data centers with debt, unlike cash-rich hyperscalers.
ZIRP — Zero interest-rate policy.
M2 / M3 — Broad money supply measures; M3 adds less liquid holdings.

Developing DEVELOPING — FRONTIER MODELS GET CHEAPER, NOT SMARTERai-infrasoftware

Smarter harnesses, same brains: why Pitis isn't expecting AGI soon

Pitis argues base-model intelligence stalled about a year ago; what improves is the scaffolding that infers intent and adds context, and the price per token.

  • Show opener: a symphony composed from scratch by Fable 5.2, a coding model that knows no music, iterating on key, harmony and melody. Pitis: interesting experiment, not a fan of AI music.
  • Cost per million tokens fell ~1,000x in three years since GPT-2; the latest step cut a price from $10 to $8. Google's Gemini Argon is $2/$10 per million input/output tokens, the new Carbon $4/$20 and set to rise after two weeks.
  • Fable 5.2 is Mythos 5.2 with brakes on cyber and biology (no CBRN); cyber-program partners get Mythos 5.2. Chinese open models Qwen 3.8 and GLM 5.3 are strong at cyber without guardrails.
  • Missing piece: understanding the world. Self-driving cars needed hundreds of millions of trips to learn it tacitly; a model told to go left at the green light still can't drive.
  • Genezio picks the smallest model that solves each task, uses all providers, has no own LLM yet, and chains a cheap yes/no classifier model. Every teammate uses AI for decks, case studies, code and marketing.
  • Open-source inference hosting is a huge market. Pitis compares Chinese open models to Linux: it came from Finland and Finland won nothing; China gains only reputation unless a company captures the value.

Strong conviction THESIS — COMPUTE IS THE NEW REAL ESTATEai-infraenergyspace

Compute and energy: the landlords of the AI economy

Both hosts agree energy is the final limit, and Pitis says whoever owns data centers wins regardless of which apps succeed on top.

  • Anthropic pays SpaceX/xAI $1.25B a month until May 2029 for compute. After critics said it shouldn't pay so much, Musk replied SpaceX can take the compute back.
  • Pitis: compute is like real estate. Tenants come and go; the landlord gets paid. Good land = next to water, a big power line and a big data cable.
  • Musk is now the biggest buyer and producer of gas turbines, and Anthropic has started buying turbines too. Natural gas will be the main power source for AI.
  • Buhnici: SpaceX listed this year and, with Tesla, reinvests everything into data centers and power; Starship exists to lift satellites and orbital data centers (solar power, space cooling, Starlink for data).
  • Musk needs telecom revenue (he's now targeting mobile operators) because Grok 5 has been delayed for months; for now the money comes from Anthropic.
  • On circular deals, Pitis's analogy: 1 RON passed among three friends counts as 3 RON in circulation, but nobody ends up richer.
  • Pitis: any serious AI lab's first research project should be cheap, sustainable electricity. The biggest invention would be a far better battery; Buhnici points to cheap sodium batteries.

Contested WATCH — AI-FOUND VULNERABILITIES NEED TRIAGEsoftwarepolicy

AI floods cyber with findings: more noise, more work for security firms

Pitis says AI finding thousands of vulnerabilities creates more demand for cyber firms, because someone has to triage which ten actually matter.

  • Microsoft reported 1,259 vulnerabilities in one day; ESET flagged Microsoft Exchange, and DNSC warned about a PHP bug. Pitis: a model can find 1,000 holes on your server and none may face the internet.
  • Pentest-Tools had a scanner out the same day. Its AI pentester won bug-bounty thanks from the US and UK defence departments and Sony; it pairs AI with deterministic tools because AI gives different results on each run.
  • Fireworks AI, Notion's model provider, disclosed an incident affecting ~1% of workspaces where secrets leaked; 35,000+ Grok Build apps users thought private were public. Meta has leaked similar artifacts.
  • TikTok reverse-engineered: a developer found 31 models tracking phone angle, location, scroll speed and whether you've dozed off; when it senses you're leaving, it serves a live stream; a keep-warm scheduler always has content ready.
  • Pitis on TikTok: it is an ad app built to turn your attention into money, not an information app, so you can't blame it; delete it if you want. Beware trojans posing as plugins that steal passwords.
  • The Timisoara computer-science dean says cyber enrolment and lab caseloads are surging.

Contested WATCH — STARSHIP FLIGHT 15, IBM 2027 LINKED CHIPS, ARTEMISspacesemis

Quantum is still PR; rockets are still steel tubes

Pitis sees IBM's new quantum chip as early and PR-driven, years from cracking encryption; space stays a race against China.

  • IBM's Nighthawk R3: 520 programmable qubits (2,034 physical, noisy) built to run 10,000 quantum operations, 10 couplers; two linked chips passing 1,000 qubits in 2027. Breaking RSA-2048 needs millions of logical operations and billions of gates.
  • Buhnici: 1,000x growth in three years would mean 10 million operations. Pitis: possible, but demos so far are embarrassing for the money spent, and post-quantum algorithms are already in a race.
  • Pitis: Nvidia won AI because neural nets moved to floating-point vectors that suit GPUs, not Intel's or AMD's CPUs. He expects quantum to end up at CPU level, not GPU.
  • SpaceX prepares Starship flight 15 with a double catch of booster and ship by Mechazilla; the stack is the height of Bucharest's Sky Tower. Pitis: no new propulsion or gravity discovery, so it is still human-level rocket science.
  • NASA under Jared Isaacman wants astronauts back on the Moon by 2028 with two competing teams and China as the target: Artemis 3 in 2027, Artemis 4 landing in 2028. Romania signed the Artemis Accords in 2022 (16th nation).
  • Starlink Mini 2: built-in battery (~4.5 h), Wi-Fi, USB-C, up to 60 W charging, no need to track satellites. Starlink now covers Uganda, which upset Buhnici's expedition friends who liked being offline.

Names and terms at a glance

Glossary & Entities

Harness — The scaffolding around a model (prompts, context, tools, intent detection) that makes it better at tasks without making the base model smarter.
Golden vote — Special founder voting rights that stop a board from removing them.
Lock-up period — Months after an IPO during which insiders and strategic investors can't sell.
Float — The share of a company actually offered for public trading.
GEO — Generative engine optimization: being found and recommended correctly by AI chatbots, the successor to SEO.
Earned vs paid media — Coverage others write about you vs ads you pay for; Pitis says both now live inside chatbot answers.
Value-based pricing — Charging by the value delivered (time saved) rather than by the cost of serving.
Ambisonics — Full-sphere audio capture that lets sound be re-oriented in post-production or for head-tracked playback.
NAION — Non-arteritic ischemic optic neuropathy, a rare loss of blood flow to the optic nerve linked to GLP-1 drugs.
Casa Verde — Romanian state grant programme for home solar and, now, home batteries.

The 10Y touched 5.34% and bounced: Fed isn't the only problem

Buyers defended the long end after a 24-year high, which Valentin reads as constructive but not final.

  • Closes: S&P 500 7,666.48 (+0.20%), Nasdaq 26,871.60 (+0.04%), Dow 50,926.74 (+0.04%). The 10Y reached 5.342% intraday (highest since 2002), the 2Y fell ~10 bps (biggest daily drop since August 2025). Morning of Oct 2: 10Y ~5.25%.

  • His thresholds: below 5.20% real relief for growth; 5.20-5.30% high but manageable stress; 5.30-5.35% critical; above 5.35% with confirmation multiple compression can accelerate.

Broadcom's up-to-$42B Anthropic financing changes the risk analysis

Bullish on volume, but vendor financing adds circular credit and concentration risk.

  • Per Anthropic's IPO documents, Broadcom agreed to up to $42B of financing for leasing its chips, ~a third of Anthropic's $125.2B five-year TPU/custom-compute commitment.
  • Anthropic is expected to become Broadcom's largest chip-design customer by 2027; Broadcom anticipates AI chip revenue of ~$115B in 2027 and ~$230B in 2028.
  • Upside view: AVGO can reach AMD's level, an 80-100% gain by end of 2028, his target for each new sum invested. Downside: if Anthropic's economics deteriorate, AVGO could go below $250.
  • Stock traded under $350 intraday, ~$347-350; $346-350 is key, below it $338-342; $360-365 needed to restart momentum.
  • He doesn't treat the financing as automatically bullish; the verdict is ROIC and cash flow. In a >5% yield world $42B demands a return.

NFP playbook: watch the 10Y, not the first Nasdaq tick

He plans to add SPCX, AVGO, RKLB, ASTS and NBIS only at prices and conditions he set.

  • Consensus ~100,000 jobs and 4.2% unemployment after 162,000 in August.
  • Goldilocks 70-120k with unemployment 4.1-4.2% and moderate wages: lower hike odds, lower 2Y/10Y, growth benefits.
  • Hot (above ~150k, unemployment 4.0-4.1%): hike back in play, 10Y can retest 5.34-5.35%. Weak (below 50k or negative, unemployment 4.3%+): bond rally first, then equities ask if the problem moved to growth, and SoFi and consumer names need scrutiny.
  • Key tell: a strong NFP with a 10Y that can't break 5.34% means bond selling is tiring; a moderate NFP with the 10Y above 5.35% means a structural fiscal problem.
  • Today's allocation across IBKR, GYW and GYW500 is aimed at SPCX, AVGO, RKLB, ASTS and NBIS, under conditions and prices he set. He adds that he buys nothing just because it is October 2.

Names and terms at a glance

Glossary & Entities

Idle GPU tax — The cost of expensive GPU hardware sitting unused, which erodes return on capex.
De-rating — A cut in the valuation multiple the market gives a stock.
Vendor financing — A supplier funds its customer so the customer can buy the supplier's products.
Term premium — Extra yield investors demand for holding long-dated bonds.
NFP (Nonfarm Payrolls) — Monthly BLS count of US jobs created or lost, excluding mainly farm work.

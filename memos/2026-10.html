The answer

In one read

October opened on where AI agents spend money (agentic commerce, crypto rails, card networks as the consensus loser) and closed on who pays for the buildout. With the 10-year near 5.2%, AI infra / neoclouds climbed to 23% of briefs by W43 on a credit scare (CoreWeave, hedge-fund degrossing), then cooled to 13% as enterprise demand gave the capex an underwriter. Crypto platforms were the month's steadiest narrative (13-21% every week, sentiment never broke), while the bank and telco clusters spiked and vanished within three weeks, mostly on one or two voices. Memory + optics is the open thread into November: written off in W40, emerging again by W44 (10%), then silent a week later.

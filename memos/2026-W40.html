Agents start spending: commerce, crypto rails and cyber defense

Covers briefs since 2026-09-03 · signals window 2026-09-03 to 2026-10-03 (56 briefs)
The answer

This week in one read

The conversation moved from building AI to where AI agents spend money. Meta's Muse launch made agentic commerce the dominant new thread: Meta flipped bullish, merchants with agent APIs (Shopify, Instacart) appeared out of nowhere, and payment middlemen drew bearish calls. Two parallel threads reinforce it: big asset managers putting money on-chain for machine payments, and cyber defense emerging as the second-order winner as open models close the gap with the frontier labs.

Direction, not precision

Insights

Agentic commerce is the new center of gravity, and it has a loser: card fees

  1. Meta went from no net view to clearly bullish (+0.7): 12 positive mentions, 0 negative, across 9 channels. The catalyst is Muse, its consumer agent.
  2. Merchants plugging into agents showed up fast. Shopify (agent API on every store) and Instacart (Muse launch partner) were both first named within the last two weeks, already on 3-4 channels each.
  3. The consumer theme jumped from 23% to 54% of briefs, the biggest topic shift in the library.
  4. The flip side is consistent: Visser and Pompliano both expect Visa/Mastercard to lose their fees as agents route around cards (~5 years). No bull on record this window.
  5. Brian Chesky (Airbnb): 'Agents are definitely the future of Silicon Valley and chatbots are not.'

Traditional finance is building the payment rails for machines

  1. BlackRock turned positive and is gaining attention (2.2×): a 'machine-native economy' paper, three funds on-chain, model portfolios tokenized via Ondo.
  2. Robinhood is bullish across 6 channels (+0.9). Its own chain earned ~$50M in two weeks; Tenev says tokenization takes over the financial system.
  3. Circle (USDC, agent credit rails) and Solana ('the L1 war is won', per Ran Neuner) are rising on the same thesis: agents need programmable money.
  4. Zcash is the early, contested edge: privacy for agent-to-agent payments (Jordi's 'tell') vs Gary Cardone calling it a 'childlike' narrative.
  5. New links in the graph: BlackRock + Robinhood, Bitcoin + Visa, Meta + Robinhood. Crypto and commerce are now discussed as one story.

Open models are closing the gap, and that arms attackers: cyber defense is the derivative trade

  1. Palo Alto Networks is the strongest early signal in the library: first named Sept 6, now on 4 channels with a ~10× lift, and newly linked to OpenAI, Anthropic, Nvidia and Alphabet. CrowdStrike follows the same path.
  2. The cause is in the same briefs: Chinese open models are ~3 months behind the frontier (Abliteration's founder), DeepSeek is rising on cheap tokens, and attackers exploit new open models within months (Dave Blundin).
  3. Friedberg expects cyber-defense budgets to 'go through the roof'. Emad: 'the internet will break next year' as agent swarms hit infrastructure.
  4. The same dynamic makes the frontier labs contested: Anthropic sentiment fell from +0.5 to +0.2 (8 bulls, 4 bears). Emad says it 'gets smashed in 2 years', and Pompliano says private labs are in more trouble than people realize, even as $2T IPO talk continues.

Where to look next

Worth a closer look

  1. Visa / Mastercard: the agent-disintermediation bear case is uncontested across three channels. Uncontested usually means either early or already priced; worth checking which.
  2. Anthropic: the most-discussed private company, with sentiment sliding while IPO expectations rise ($2T, after the midterms). The gap between narrative and valuation is the story to track.
  3. Amazon: cooling from strongly bullish (+0.9) to mixed (+0.4) on the Muse lock-out question. Chris Camillo flags next earnings as the test of Muse's impact on Bedrock and chips.
  4. Semis and space losing share of the conversation: semis fell from 47% to 36% of briefs and space from 23% to 12%, while Dan Ives says chip equilibrium isn't until late 2028. Falling attention against a still-rising thesis can be a contrarian setup.
  5. Zcash and Instinct AI: both new, both contested, both tied to the agent economy. Early enough that the next two weeks of mentions will show whether they spread or fade.

Banks move onto crypto rails while the model layer gets cheap

Covers briefs since 2026-10-03 · signals window 2026-09-08 to 2026-10-08 (92 briefs)
The answer

This week in one read

Two repricings are running in parallel. Payments: card networks are the one consensus loser of the agent era (Visa and Mastercard both ~-0.75, zero positive mentions), while banks (JPMorgan, Citi, BNY, Morgan Stanley) show up building on Solana and tokenization rails. Models: frontier competence is now cheap enough that vertical AI margins are the new worry, and operators on stage say the real blocker is evals, drift and ownership, not the model. Software names are coming back into view on that second story.

Hayes: AI compute contracts trigger the money-printing collision within 18 months

AI productivity is real but too slow to outrun sovereign debt, so central banks print, and crypto and gold win.

  • Trigger to watch: France "is going to blow up soon", forcing central bankers to start "hammering that brrr button".
  • The Kevin Warsh / Scott Bessent theory that AI productivity pays down national debt: Hayes says AI agents will "take over commerce" but not fast enough to stop a debt implosion without printing.
  • The collision: OpenAI, Anthropic and SpaceX signed for trillions of compute payable on delivery; data centers land late 2027 to early 2028, then labs must raise hundreds of billions, fail to raise, or prove positive unit economics.
  • Trade: long crypto and gold as money-printing hedges; don't short AI stocks ("very difficult"); Bitcoin is crypto's beta, plus maybe Ethereum and cash-flowing dapps.
  • His project Flop: a Bitcoin-style currency mined by doing useful GPU inference for agents; public testnet late October to early Q1 airdrops 25% of 10-year supply, genesis block in Q1 next year.

Murad: the winning memecoin cult goes past $1 trillion

As AI makes skills abundant, value shifts to belief and community, and tokenized movements capture it.

  • Value arc: 10,000 years ago the body mattered, in 2026 IQ matters, next the bottleneck is EQ, spirituality and the "relational economy".
  • A billion AI agents come on chain and beat humans at any trade horizon under ~2-3 weeks, pushing humans to extreme long holds and community building.
  • Names for it: humanist premium, social premium, belief theory of value; a token is "a donation basket with a megaphone attached".
  • Two markets now: hyper-gambling launchpads (hundreds of thousands of coins a day, holds in seconds, measured by Pump.fun and FOMO launch counts) vs hardcore cults holding 10 years aiming at 10x Doge.
  • Next $200-300B memecoin is a "hardcore tokenized ideology"; investing and spirituality merge, and that is where the next 1,000x is. The host's own panel on memecoins paired with stocks called that pairing an innovation.

Quantum and AI attacks put Bitcoin's upgrade clock and Zcash's privacy pitch center stage

A quantum warning from Justin Drake soured the mood; Bitcoin's slow consensus makes 2030 tight, and privacy projects pitch themselves as the answer.

  • Justin Drake's viral tweet warning people to prepare for a quantum break day "really did dampen the mood".
  • Muneeb Ali (Stacks): Bitcoin devs got early access to Anthropic's Mythos model in private beta and audited Bitcoin Core with it; it came out clean.
  • Ali: upgrades take ~2 years to ship (SegWit, Taproot) and migrating coins another ~2 years on Bitcoin's bandwidth, so starting now already reaches the 2030 window; the community is split on whether to act.
  • Ali's plan: fork a proof-of-concept Bitcoin with quantum signatures to show the market their size; a 2013 holder, he'll start exiting BTC if there's no progress by 2028.
  • Cypherpunk Technologies (Winklevoss-backed, CIO Will McEvoy): owns ~2% of Zcash supply and mines just under 20% of the network; AI pointed at public ledgers makes private assets necessary.
  • McEvoy: Zcash is cheaper, faster, private and quantum-resistant Bitcoin with easier-to-coordinate dev teams; worth 10%, maybe 20%, of BTC vs ~2% today. Next stop ZCON in Cancun.
  • Floor sightings: Cardano's Midnight privacy layer.

Robinhood chain is the proof case for Ethereum layer 2s and value accrual

Robinhood's chain, built on Arbitrum's stack, is the fastest and most successful EVM launch, and it routes fees back to ARB holders.

  • Robinhood's Johan: 70-72% of tokenized-stock volume happens on weekends or after hours; anyone with a phone can get exposure to US markets.
  • Steven Goldfeder (Arbitrum): Robinhood chain leads EVM chains on transaction volume, assets on chain and revenue already collected.
  • Why not build its own chain: Arbitrum's stack gives Ethereum security, 100 ms block times no other network matches natively, and Stylus (Rust, C, C++ contracts) for enterprises.
  • Value accrual: the ARB holder wallet gets fees from all Arbitrum One transactions plus 10% of Robinhood chain fees and of any L2 built on the stack.
  • Goldfeder's prediction: 5-10 ecosystems as successful as Robinhood's on Arbitrum within two years, from banks and fintechs in the US and globally.

Ondo's edge in tokenized equities is liquidity sourced from TradFi

On-chain liquidity is expensive, so Ondo quotes against the real TradFi order book instead of prefunding DEX pools.

  • Started with tokenized T-bills, a bear-market hit when DeFi yields fell and hacks rose; bills sit at a custodian.
  • Liquidity is the hidden RWA problem: the ability to sell when you want matters more than buying.
  • RFQ model: a user sends $1M USDC, Ondo prices off the TradFi order book, sends Tesla tokens and buys the underlying shares at its broker.
  • Competitors prefund AMM/DEX pools; Ondo's added assets don't consume liquidity, so it lists 600+ assets with deep liquidity.
  • Ondo is now partnering with BlackRock.

Names and terms at a glance

Glossary & Entities

Golden cross — When a 50-day moving average crosses above the 200-day, read as a bullish trend signal.
RFQ (request for quote) — A trader asks for a price and the provider fills from its own sourced liquidity instead of a pooled AMM.
Social premium — Murad's term for value that comes from belief and community rather than technology or cash flow.
Stylus — Arbitrum feature allowing contracts in Rust, C and C++ alongside EVM code.
Quantum signatures — Post-quantum cryptographic signatures, far larger than Bitcoin's current ones, needed to resist quantum attacks.

AMD's record Q3 and $17B guide confirm AI demand, with one engine carrying the growth

He calls the print fundamentally positive, but wants regular-session volume, not the 23:00 enthusiasm, to confirm it.

  • Revenue $13.377B (+45% y/y); Data Center $8.5B (+97%); GAAP net income $2.428B (+95%), GAAP EPS $1.46 (+95%), adjusted EPS $2.09 (+74%); adjusted gross margin 56%, up 2 points.

  • The weaker part: Client and Gaming revenue fell 8% y/y to $3.7B, so a business leaning on one engine needs it to run flawlessly every quarter.
  • Tuesday regular close $483.90 (+3.65%); after-hours indication near $508 (~5% higher), which he says often evaporates in the first 30 minutes of the next session.
  • Levels: support $473-480 (Tuesday's range); resistance $497-500, then $508-513.

Oracle rebounds, but the $20B financing puts dilution and 7-8.65% debt on the clock

Demand for Oracle's compute is real; the risk is timing between building data centers and collecting multi-year contract revenue.

  • Per Associated Press and Reuters, Democrats won Senate races in Texas, South Carolina, North Carolina and Ohio and made governor gains; control of both chambers is not final and results are projections, not legal certification.
  • First market reaction: US index futures down, Asian AI stocks under pressure, dollar weaker, Treasuries up (yields down), which prices a less friendly Congress and more tech regulation.

Rotation JENSEN HUANG RESIGNS · NOV 2026semisai-infra

Jensen Huang's exit pushes semis money from Nvidia to AMD and Broadcom

Dragu reads the departure as a durable hit to Nvidia's valuation that benefits AMD and Broadcom and drags Nvidia-dependent names.

  • Dragu has been adding to Broadcom; he also holds Nebius, which he says is hit despite a strong report because of its Nvidia ties.
  • Nvidia's index weight touches ETFs, 401(k)s and pension funds, so the impact is "very hard", not one man's mistake quickly forgotten.
  • Nvidia market cap about $4T; Alex Marian sees maybe $6T upside (~50%), arguing semis peers carry less risk for the same exposure.
  • Dragu: Nvidia trades at a very low multiple, but semis growth has 3-4 more quarters before the market prices decelerating growth (40% to 36% to 32%).
  • The stock is moving from growth investors to value investors, who "negotiate everything" like an apartment buyer haggling below the cheapest listing.
  • Example of a good company at a bad price: buy Nvidia near $230 and a consolidation at $140-170 leaves you down 20-25% for a long time.
  • Huang owns about 3.5-3.6% of Nvidia; Elon Musk said he'd hire him, and the hosts muse a SpaceX-for-Nvidia share swap.

Bullish COREWEAVE + NEBIUS EARNINGSai-infrafinance

CoreWeave's strong print meets a bond market charging 15%

Both neoclouds reported well; the risk sits in the credit market, where CoreWeave's borrowing costs and default insurance have jumped.

  • CoreWeave backlog rose to about $100B from about $70B (contracts signed in advance).
  • Its traded loans yield around 15%, which weighs on future borrowing; rising Treasury yields raise the cost of convertibles and senior notes.
  • Default insurance on CoreWeave debt has climbed sharply; lenders are paying up to hedge.
  • Nebius rose 16-18% right after CoreWeave's report; Dragu calls its balance sheet much stronger than CoreWeave's.
  • Nebius stopped giving forward guidance, but both firms have large prepaid contracts.

SoftBank's pledged ARM shares are a margin call waiting for a further drop

With about 70% of the portfolio in ARM and OpenAI and ARM shares pledged for loans, SoftBank is hoping, and hope is not a strategy.

  • SoftBank reported a $7B net loss, mostly unrealised, after ARM's fall.
  • It pledged ARM (and OpenAI) shares to banks to borrow and invest more in OpenAI; Dragu thinks the pledge level is around $280-300+ per ARM share, but isn't sure.
  • Alex Marian puts a further 25-35% ARM drop as the trigger for forced cuts; Dragu says selling would just push ARM lower and invite execution by the banks.
  • Masayoshi Son built his record on near-zero-rate yen swapped into dollars; he lost 70-80% on SoFi, which the hosts made 300% on.
  • Dragu would have preferred Son bet on SpaceX or even Anthropic over OpenAI.
  • Their view of Son: at the end of his career he wants to make history, not be right, which makes him dangerous with other people's money.

Anthropic's IPO keeps the AI money flowing, but it won't outshine SpaceX

Its backers need a successful listing to mark up their stakes; the hosts doubt the hype and rank SpaceX's IPO far above it.

  • Pricing about $78/share, targeting $1.2-1.4T market cap and raising $60-80B; valuation talk fell from $2T to $1-1.2T within a month.
  • Amazon: about $18B invested, about 15x gain, stake worth about $271B; Google: about $33B invested plus $10B at the IPO, stake about $139B.
  • Announced IPO buyers: Google $10B, Nvidia $10B, AMD $2.5B, Broadcom and Microsoft about $1B each, TSMC $500M.
  • Dragu: a successful listing sustains AI investment; a weak one makes banks and VC funds ask whether to keep funding AI, risking a big AI correction.
  • Dragu would have delayed the listing to summer 2027, as he says Sam Altman did with OpenAI.
  • Alex Marian cites an IPO watcher who can't see the hype: Claude Code writing code is real productivity, but the AI-replaces-everything fear is overdone.
  • AI products commoditise in 4-6 months (Chinese or US rivals make today's paid feature free), while each new model needs costlier Nvidia/AMD compute.
  • Next, Alex expects OpenAI to list sooner than expected, pulling liquidity from wherever Anthropic investors take profits.

Names and terms at a glance

Glossary & Entities

Neocloud — A specialist GPU cloud provider (CoreWeave, Nebius) renting AI compute to model builders.
Exit liquidity — Retail buying that lets large holders sell a position without crashing the price.
Tax-loss selling — Selling losing positions before year end to offset gains for tax purposes.
Producer prices (PPI) — Prices received by producers; an early signal of where consumer inflation is heading.
Pledged shares — Stock posted as loan collateral; lenders can sell it if the price falls below a set level.

Names and terms at a glance

Glossary & Entities

Macro seasons — Raoul's mapping of business and liquidity cycles onto seasons, with summer meaning risk on.
Everything code — Raoul's thesis that global liquidity, driven by debt refinancing, explains most asset prices.
Debt refi cycle — The roughly 5.6-year rhythm of government and corporate debt rollovers that drives liquidity.
Stretch score — Blended Z-score of 14-day RSI versus its 3-month trend, used to flag overbought or oversold assets.
Trend valuation — Fair value measured as distance from trend and from the everything-code level, not P/E.
Compounding machine — A backtester for standard-deviation buy and sell rules on Bitcoin and the Nasdaq.

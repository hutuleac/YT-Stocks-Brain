Names and terms at a glance

Glossary & Entities

Dow theory — Trend model that signals a bull market when price breaks above prior swing highs after a decline.
Positive (bullish) divergence — Price makes a lower low while an oscillator like MACD makes a higher low, hinting that selling is fading.
MACD — Moving-average convergence divergence, a momentum indicator built from two exponential moving averages.
Golden ratio extensions — Fibonacci targets (138.2%, 161.8%) projecting how far a breakout may run.
200 SMA — 200-period simple moving average, a common long-term trend line.
Double bottom — Two lows at roughly the same level, read as a potential reversal.

TikTok Shop affiliate: sell other people's products for 15-30% commission

No website or inventory — you post videos about a product and earn commission on sales through your link; Danny says the average serious creator makes $15-30K a month.

  • Not TikTok Shop seller and not drop shipping: commission is 15-25%, sometimes 30%, depending on the brand.
  • Claimed range: $15-30K a month for 'the average person', with multiple friends at six figures a month.
  • Workload: 3-6 videos a day (2-4 hours including research), or live streams at $60-100 an hour (but that's still trading time).
  • Since GMV Max launched ~6 months ago, products need a brand spending on ads to get picked up — pick reputable or proven sellers. Early (late 2023-24) anything could go viral.
  • Research with Kalodata (he says he isn't affiliated): look for rising revenue trends and products that solve a problem or an insecurity — supplements, skincare, vacuums.
  • Downside: violations and bans you can't control — 'don't make any claims', and if you think you shouldn't say it, don't.

The best ads don't look like ads: Danny's six-step video formula

Speak to one specific person's problem, earn the next 3 seconds, and show the product last — by the end they should already want it.

  • Lose them in the first 3 seconds and you've lost. Four hook layers: verbal, on-screen text, format (skit, talking head, text) — the verbal hook is the least important.
  • Order: hook, problem, relatability (you or a relative with the problem), credibility, how life changed, then the product. Holding the product up in the first seconds screams ad.
  • Write for one ideal client persona: 'this is how it really feels to have acne' beats 'this will make your skin clear'.
  • Niche beats reach: 300,000 views on the exact buyer outsell millions of untargeted ones; a lung product aimed at COPD patients converts because nobody else targets them.
  • Sell the problem, not the feature — the pheromone cologne that sold out promised attraction, not a nice smell. For cologne: 'How to tell if your natural smell is repulsive to women'.
  • Brad's example: the $25 cologne that out-compliments $500 bottles of Creed Aventus, Tom Ford or Louis Vuitton, delivered like a tip to a buddy.
  • Going viral = consistency plus iterations: each post is feedback; copy the outlier that got 1,200 views instead of 500.

Recommendation USER-GENERATED CONTENTcareer

UGC retainers: brands pay ~$100 a video for ads that look organic

UGC gives a steady base that survives a TikTok ban; TikTok Shop is the training ground that proves you can sell.

  • Typical retainer: $3,000 for 30 videos; Danny's deals were ~$4K for 30, each taking ~10 minutes.
  • Videos usually run on the brand's account; viewers find the product via a ManyChat DM link, a quick shot of the site, or planted comments asking 'what's the name of that site?'
  • When his TikTok Shop accounts were banned for a month his income fell from ~$15K to zero; a $4K UGC base covered the bills.
  • Getting work: prove you can go viral, build a media kit of your best videos, email brands (even via an AI agent) or agencies, or apply on Tribe (Meta ads; he knows the founder, Lucas).
  • Brad's friend listed himself on Fiverr and made $5-6K making UGC videos.
Pull quote
“A good UGC creator, you don't know that they're making ads. That's that's kind of the art of UGC.”— Danny Fisher
Worth noting
The tactics described (no link, planted comments) are designed to hide that a video is paid; US FTC rules require disclosing paid endorsements.

Principle MINDSETmindsetcareer

Fear staying put more than change — and stop consuming other people's limits

Danny credits the switch to fearing 'what happens if I don't change', cutting unintentional scrolling and news, and taking full accountability.

  • Two fears: fear of change and judgment ('people you wouldn't trade lives with') vs fear of not changing. The second made him act.
  • Focus: his hours went to the job, music and the gym, none toward the life he wanted; he dropped everything but one thing.
  • Intentional consumption: an hour or two of scrolling a week, only to study; hit 'not interested' on news and on anyone whose life he didn't aspire to. Alex Hormozi: never easier to make money, never harder to focus.
  • Read Think and Grow Rich (Napoleon Hill) and 'actually believed it'; listened to Bezos and Elon Musk instead of coworkers.
  • Both call college a scam that 'breeds employees'; social commerce is ~$2.6 trillion and still 'the gold rush', like Gary Vee's early wine YouTube.
  • Don't quit blind: build income on the side or save 3-6 months; 'a job's always going to be available'. Brad read James McCrae's 'Instructions before visiting Earth'.

Names and terms at a glance

Glossary & Entities

TikTok Shop affiliate — Creators earning commission on sales of other brands' products through in-video links.
UGC — User-generated content: organic-looking videos made for brands, often posted on the brand's account.
GMV Max — TikTok Shop's ad product; brands spending on it get their products pushed, helping affiliates.
ICP — Ideal client persona — the one specific viewer a video is written for.
TAM — Total addressable market.
Media kit — A creator's portfolio of best-performing videos sent to brands.

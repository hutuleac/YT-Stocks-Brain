Framework THEORY TO PRACTICE, 2022-2026cryptogeopolitics

Cities, not just companies and currencies, are the new competitive unit

The ultimate real-world asset is a community, a city, even a country.

  • Network State (book, 2022) asked whether giant social networks and crypto are the end of the road, or can be printed out physically.
  • Valuing New York: ~8.5M people x $300-400K real estate per person gives roughly $2-3 trillion; the largest companies, liquidated and rebuilt in one place, land at a similar scale.
  • Implications: a possible city asset (an "NYC or SF" token), and valuing China by Shanghai and Shenzhen as well as its tech companies.
  • Astana overtook San Francisco 10 years ago on growth and builds ~100x the housing; Kazakhstan is also building Alau Town, billed as the first crypto city.
  • A city need not sit in one place: Hilton's ~1 million beds, offices and restaurants worldwide are a "network city".

Personal story NETWORK SCHOOL, 2024-2026cryptopolicy

Network School proved a community can be printed out, then moved

Bring thousands of people to one place, then relocate them as liquidly as in the digital world, with some friction.

  • Opened 2024 in a Malaysian ghost city on an island off Singapore; people from 80+ countries attended.
  • Malaysia stopped wanting them; Kazakhstan offered "an amazing deal" with full state support and attendance records followed.
  • Also moved the community to Próspera, and says offers came from 10 countries; the transcript also mentions a Costa Rica move and a Republic of Costa deal.
  • Analogy: when China shut down Bitcoin mining, Bitcoin redistributed to other nodes — the same robustness, now physical.
  • Peers named: Vitalik's Zuzalu and Bryan Johnson's Don't Die community, as networks of pop-ups that become permanent.

The build playbook: cloud-first, pop-ups, distressed real estate

Start online, prove demand with pop-ups, then lease or buy already-built, under-used real estate.

  • Cloud-first, land-last: begin as a Substack or Telegram group motivated by something more than money.
  • Pop-ups are the new startups: print the cloud out for a limited time (Cursed Cafe, AngelList cafe, Coinbase taking over a small town), then build a waitlist for a permanent site.
  • Capital online, build offline — like Elon de-risking Tesla with an online waitlist; crowd-fund the community in crypto.
  • Distressed real estate: buy abandoned or de-industrialized towns; Japan has ~9 million empty homes; Magdalene College is 129 acres for sale.
  • Frontier, not fancy: recruit people who build like Habitat for Humanity, not guests of a resort; the East has seen cities built from nothing (a Chinese city shown 1984 vs 2023), the West less so.
Framework MEMBERSHIP, MODEL, METRICcryptomindset

Designing the society: circles, one commandment, subscription, man-months

A startup society needs a reason to exist, tiered commitment, a business model and its own scoreboard.

  • Concentric circles: core team (core devs), long-termers (maximalists), members (holders), visitors — a continuum of engagement.
  • One commandment: select for a purpose beyond airdrops — vegan, language immersion, longevity, internet off at 9pm, carb-free; selective admission is legitimate like a college's.
  • Society as a service: the Google campus as a subscription (food, room, gym, events); bulk buying makes it "crypto Costco", a DAO in the physical world.
  • Identity = community = security = territory: token-gated digital access should match the physical checkpoint; some spaces must be members-only.
  • Upside offsets utility: like early Bitcoin or WhatsApp, a tiny network runs on upside; a "crypto equity" for communities is teased.
  • Man-months = people x months stayed, the physical analogue of coins x price; generalizes the census for nomads and is hard to fake with proof of human and proof of location.

Counterintuitive take LEVERAGE CLAIMgeopoliticspolicy

Collective exit replaces the military

A mobile tribe of tech people can bargain with states by threatening to sign with someone else.

  • Answer to "nation-states have militaries": Google and Bitcoin reached trillions without firing a shot.
  • Organized, premeditated flight beats a pointless fight; the Puritans' collective exit built the US.
  • Network School had offers from 10 countries and took the best; he says this leverage was theoretical a year ago and is now proven.
  • Frames it as unbundling the sovereign individual and rebundling a sovereign collective.
Worth noting
Critics' objection (no military, states will crush it) is answered by the speaker's own experience only.

Names and terms at a glance

Glossary & Entities

Network state — A community that starts online, coordinates around a shared purpose, then crowd-funds and occupies physical territory.
Man-months — Number of people times the months each stays in a society, proposed as the physical-world equivalent of market cap.
Society as a service — A subscription providing housing, food, gym and events, like a company campus.
Collective exit — A community relocating together to a more favorable jurisdiction instead of fighting.

# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Primary: the owner (Peter). Uses it to recall what a specific video said and to spot investing ideas before they go mainstream. Reads on desktop and phone equally.

Secondary: investors and curious readers who land on the public GitHub Pages site. Welcome, but the site is not optimized for them first.

## Product Purpose

A library of structured research briefs generated from YouTube videos (investing/AI-news podcasts, market interviews, dev/workflow and life content). It turns hours of video into scannable, themed briefs and builds toward a knowledge graph over stocks, investing, and industry trends.

Success: finding what a video said in seconds, and seeing converging narratives early.

## Positioning

Briefs are organized by theme, not by fixed tables, and feed one shared knowledge base (entities, claims, relations). The Signals tab surfaces narratives as clusters of co-mentioned entities across briefs, not prediction scores.

## Operating Context

- Static HTML, no build step, no dependencies. Hosted on GitHub Pages from `main`.
- Every brief is generated by `scripts/generate.py` from a per-video data file; `index.html`, Signals, and memos are regenerated on every run. Design changes go into the generator, not hand-edited pages.
- Surfaces: `index.html` (tabs: All Briefs, By Channel, By Company/Ticker, Quotes & Takes, Signals, Dev & Workflows, Life & Perspectives, Romania), per-brief pages, weekly/monthly memos in `memos/`.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Categories: `market`, `dev`, `life`; `region: "ro"` for Romanian videos (brief in English, quotes verbatim Romanian).
- Market briefs use stance/conviction vocabulary; non-market briefs use descriptive "character of the claim" language. Never mix.
- Sponsor content never appears.
- No RISKS section, no prediction grading, no audit vocabulary ("unverified"), never tell the reader to go verify.
- Signals: market briefs only, windows counted in briefs, no contested/gaining/turning lenses.
- SEO must be preserved; layouts must be mobile-friendly.

## Brand Commitments

Name: YT Stocks Brain. Owner preference: clean, minimal, Apple-ecosystem feel; dark-mode comfortable; low-scroll, page-width layouts.

## Evidence on Hand

Real briefs (root `*.html`, `library.json`, `research-data/`), knowledge base (`kb/`), memos (`memos/`). No testimonials, user counts, or performance track records exist; do not fabricate them.

## Product Principles

1. Recall speed first: what a video said, found in seconds.
2. Direction over precision: show trends and early signals, never score predictions.
3. Provenance over judgment: label who said what; don't grade it.
4. One generator, one system: every page comes from the same templates.
5. Readable on any screen without losing density.

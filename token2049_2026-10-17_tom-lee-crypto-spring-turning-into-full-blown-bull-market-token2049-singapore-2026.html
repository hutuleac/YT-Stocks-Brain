Five signals that the crypto bull market has already started

Institutions, technicals, flows and relative performance all point to a bull market that is easy to doubt because it still feels fragile.

  • Institutions are declaring it: Fidelity, BlackRock and other majors; he has not seen that in the past three crypto winters.
  • Technical: Bitcoin closed above its 200-day average on Aug 19 and Ethereum had a golden cross the same day; of the last nine such crosses, eight started bull markets.
  • Since 2010 Bitcoin averages about 80% positivity and 200% gains over the following 6 months; Ethereum averages 290%.
  • Positive ETF flows only happen in bull markets; crypto was the best-performing macro asset in Q3 (June-September) while AI stocks fell.
  • That came despite a hawkish Fed, surging oil, rising global yields and tight conditions; the four-year cycle bottomed October 5.

Why this cycle is bigger: tokenization, wealth transfer, DATs and AI

Earlier booms were retail stories (ICOs, NFTs, stablecoins); this one has institutions, inheritance and treasuries behind it.

  • Tokenization: BlackRock and other managers want to tokenize everything; Vlad Tenev says "everything running on traditional financial rails will be tokenized" and sees a supercycle.
  • Scale: crypto is a ~$2T market against ~$200T of liquid assets growing $7T a year; each $1T in is a 1-to-37 ratio, and the top three markets hold $160T.
  • BlackRock, JPMorgan and Robinhood build on Ethereum, whose developer count equals the rest of the community combined.
  • Wealth transfer: Tenev cites $100T over 10 years; IRS data shows $400B inherited in the last bull market versus ~$1.5-2T next cycle, with at least $12T leaving private equity and debt.
  • Digital asset treasuries: ETH DATs hold 7% of supply, BTC DATs 6.5%, SOL DATs 3%, Hyperliquid DATs 14%; they pull supply from the market in a bull run.
  • AI link: a16z calls agentic payments the grand unification of AI and crypto; agents will carry wallets, and Mag 7, software and crypto outperformed as semis and memory corrected.

BitMine: 6M ETH, a 5% hard cap and a record buyback

Lee argues BitMine is built to outperform Ethereum now that it has stopped accumulating.

  • Holds 6 million ETH (4.9% of supply), bought in 15 months in a bear market, much at around $1,500; needs ~100,000 more to hit a 5% hard cap Vitalik and the Foundation asked for.
  • Added to the Russell 1000, one of only six crypto stocks and the only Ethereum play; only four DATs trade above NAV, BitMine among them.
  • Performance: June 30-December 2025 ETH +22%, BitMine +500%; in the 2026 bear market BitMine +3% vs ETH -9%.
  • Buyback: about $350M from July 14 over a month, the largest crypto buyback ever; the stock rose after it stopped.
  • BMNP perpetual preferred issued at $80, now ~$100, up 24% while long Treasuries fell 8%; yields 9.5% vs ~12% for Bitcoin preferreds, ~15% for Solana.
  • Other assets: Maven staking at about $1M a day, a stake in Eightco (holds an OpenAI stake and Worldcoin) and a stake in Beast Industries.

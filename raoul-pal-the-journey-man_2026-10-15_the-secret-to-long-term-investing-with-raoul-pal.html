Core method HOW HE ALLOCATES — SECULAR TREND FIRSTcryptofinance

The "compounding machine": log regression channels on Bitcoin and the Nasdaq

Pick assets in long secular uptrends, then use how far they sit from their log trend to decide when to add and when to trim.

  • Old hedge-fund process: 1-6 month setups, risk budget of ~3% of NAV per trade, total stops capped at a 10-15% monthly drawdown. He came to hate getting stopped out.
  • Bitcoin buy signals at 1 and 1.5 SD oversold "work really well"; exits sit at up to 2 SD overbought so he stays with the trend.
  • "Not a trading indicator": weekly and monthly versions, for major allocation calls only.
  • Build it yourself: ask Claude or ChatGPT for a TradingView log-regression indicator in Pine Script.
  • For people who want to trade the wiggles: the weekly GMI trend indicator on TradingView (green/red dots) shows the interim trend vs. the secular one.

Risk management SIZE BY VOLATILITY, NOT CONVICTIONcryptofinance

Volatility is the sizing tool most people forget

An 85%-volatility small cap or small crypto can take you down 70-80% before you notice, so position size has to follow volatility.

  • Nasdaq 100 runs at a "normal" 16% volatility; Solana at 59%.
  • Bitcoin is more volatile than the S&P 500, so it gets a smaller weight unless you deliberately overweight it.
  • Diversification helps less than people think: everything goes risk-on/risk-off together because liquidity drives it.
  • Expect drawdowns of 30-40% in crypto and ~15% in the Nasdaq even inside secular uptrends.
  • Keep some cash for dislocations, which come about once a year; he added in the "Crypto 10/10" crash and over the summer.

Names and terms at a glance

Glossary & Entities

Log regression channel — A trend line fitted to the log of price, with standard-deviation bands showing how stretched an asset is versus its long-run trend.
CAGR — Compound annual growth rate; here the yearly return implied by the slope of the log trend.
DeMark 9 / 13 — Tom DeMark's sequential counts that flag likely trend exhaustion and reversal points.
Risk budget — The share of NAV you accept losing across all stop-losses combined, e.g. 3% per trade, 10-15% per month in total.
ISM — The US manufacturing survey Pal uses as his business-cycle gauge.
GMI macro seasons — Global Macro Investor's map of business-cycle and liquidity phases to the asset classes that historically do best in each.
